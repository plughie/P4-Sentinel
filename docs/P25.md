# P25 activity monitoring

P4 Sentinel listens to a selected P25 control-channel candidate, validates the network identifier, and decodes supported CRC-valid TSBKs. It reports group channel assignments as activity observations: the assigned RF channel, the control frequency, NAC, and talkgroup when present. It does not retune to the assigned channel, determine whether voice is actually present there, decode audio, or defeat encryption. A grant is evidence of an assignment, not proof of a nearby person or agency.

## Road-trip scan plan

The default `San Mateo → South Lake Tahoe` plan cycles published control-channel candidates from the San Mateo County sites, the CRIS/EBRCS East Bay and Sacramento corridor, CRIS Pine Hill and Leviathan Peak, and PIRCS Tahoe Basin/Genoa Peak sites. The plan is intentionally a shortlist, not an exhaustive statewide scan. Frequency/site databases can become stale; use decoded NAC/site details and live reception to confirm what is actually on air. The plan assumes a Bay Area → Sacramento → US-50 drive and does not use GPS.

The scan area is selected manually; the app does not request GPS or infer a county. Separate San Mateo, Contra Costa, Alameda, Sacramento, El Dorado, and Tahoe Basin plans are available. `Manual · 770 MHz` listens without cycling at the operator-entered center frequency.

## Decoder limits

- Phase 1 C4FM symbol slicing, P25 frame sync and BCH/parity-valid NID are supported.
- TSBKs use status-symbol removal, inverse interleaving, rate-1/2 trellis Viterbi decoding, and CRC-16 validation.
- Group voice grant (0x00), group grant update (0x02/0x03), and identifier updates (0x33 TDMA, 0x34 VHF/UHF, 0x3D FDMA) are handled for frequency mapping. Phase II slot counts are used to resolve the carrier frequency; the app still does not follow or decode Phase II voice. Unsupported MFIDs/opcodes and assignments without a learned channel identifier are ignored.
- Channel assignments are observations only. The tuner stays on the selected control channel; no voice channel following or audio decoding is performed.
- The current demodulator uses a simple C4FM hard slicer. CQPSK/LSM simulcast reception, frequency-offset correction, and reliable decoding in weak/multipath conditions remain unverified. A road test is needed before calling field reception complete.
- Scanning one frequency at a time creates gaps. The route preset cycles every listed candidate; short transmissions can be missed between visits.

## Frequency references

The preset combines frequencies listed as control channels for the San Mateo County P25 Regional Communications System and regional sites with corridor candidates. San Mateo local site lists are documented in [La Honda](https://www.police-frequencies.com/california/san-mateo/la-honda/) and [Pescadero](https://www.police-frequencies.com/california/san-mateo/pescadero/), and the system is indexed by [RadioReference](https://www.radioreference.com/db/sid/6919). East Bay route candidates include [Mt. Diablo CRIS and CCCO Central/West EBRCS](https://www.police-frequencies.com/california/contra-costa/). The Tahoe candidates come from the [South Lake Tahoe listing](https://www.police-frequencies.com/california/el-dorado/south-lake-tahoe/). Downtown Sacramento and Pine Hill entries are listed in [Sacramento](https://www.police-frequencies.com/california/sacramento/sacramento/) and [Lockwood](https://www.police-frequencies.com/california/amador/lockwood/) site data. These are community-maintained frequency listings, not a guarantee of current control-channel assignment or coverage.

CRIS is a California OES statewide trunked interoperable system. The [official CRIS page](https://www.caloes.ca.gov/office-of-the-director/operations/public-safety-communications/radio-communications-branch/california-radio-interoperable-system-cris/) provides system context; it is not the frequency source for every preset entry.
